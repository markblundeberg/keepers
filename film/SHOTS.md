# Shot list (draft 1, 29 Oct 2026; the voice track will retime it)

Times are film seconds. VO lines are printed words only (TREATMENT.md).
Kits: A engine/print/type, B light and place, C birds; D (later) chart and
calendar for movements II and III.

## I. One lantern

| # | time | shot | VO / type | kits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-7 | Black plate. Sea, wind, the tick of the lens clockwork. At 3 s, from far out at sea, one point of light flashes once (the beam faces us), then nothing. | type, small, lower left: *South Bishop Lighthouse* / *21 October 1887* | A, B |
| 2 | 7-18 | Middle distance from the sea: the rock, the tower, the beam turning through mist (half a turn in 11 s). The first specks in the beam. | VO 1: *"Hundreds of birds of all sorts,"* | A, B, C |
| 3 | 18-32 | Close, from below the gallery (the painting's angle). The beam blazes past; the cone full of birds; dark shapes against the glazing; strikes (dry taps). A tally in fives builds at the frame edge, as printed marks. | VO 1 cont.: *"flying around the lantern, from eleven P.M. to two-thirty A.M."* | A, B, C |
| 4 | 32-38 | At the glazing: one bird strikes and drops out of frame; the beam moves on; another comes. | none | B, C |
| 5 | 38-47 | Dawn: the plate burnished pale grey. The gallery and the rock, small still shapes, a wing out of place. Nothing moves but mist. | VO 2: *"Several birds seen on the rock at daylight with broken wings."* | A, B, C |
| 6 | 47-53 | The count, set in type over the dawn plate, as the report printed it. | type: *272 killed, viz., "Larks," 146; Starlings, 64; Blackbirds, 38 (caught three old males); Thrushes, 24.* VO 3 (optional): *"two hundred and seventy-two killed"* | A |
| 7 | 53-57 | A leg and a wing laid on a printed envelope; a schedule beside it. | type: *"in the envelopes provided for this purpose"* (committee, 1886) | A, C (still life) |

## III. Laid together

| # | time | shot | VO / type | kits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 75-92 | The darkest plate. Nine years of nights, one column a night: each column's tone set by the moon's light at the stations (lit fraction above the horizon), and every strike night a tally tick. The ticks gather in the dark bands. | cards: *Strikes on 3 in 100 moonlit nights; 8 in 100 dark ones.* / *No keeper could see it from one rock. ## Open questions

- The keeper never appears. (Deliberate: we have his words, not his face;
  the film is what he saw.)
- Sound: whether any music at all, or only sea, clockwork, taps and voices.
  Mark judges.
- Length: 102 s; a 60 s social cut could be shots 2-3, 5-6, 8, 9-10.
