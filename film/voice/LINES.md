# Line sheet for tsubomi (29 Oct 2026; script lock for the voice)

Every line is printed in the British Association's *Report on the Migration
of Birds* for 1887. They are the committee's transcriptions of the keepers'
schedules, so a keeper reading "his" entry is our staging, not a recording.
The `spoken` column is the printed text with figures spoken as words and
nothing else changed.

**Pace for all:** unhurried, plain, a man reading his own logbook aloud to
himself, late. No performance, no gravitas, no sorrow in the voice; the
words do that. A small pause at each semicolon. Not slow-dramatic: think of
reading out a tally.

**Accents** follow who kept the light (data/keepers.csv, 1887) or which
service ran it. Each lighthouse service moved keepers between stations, so
an accent is a plausible choice, not a fact. Say so in the credits.

**Auditions:** one line per role, 3-4 voices each: sb1, md1, ed1, cg1, cr1,
tk1. Mark picks by ear. Then the final takes, 3 each, of all eight lines.
